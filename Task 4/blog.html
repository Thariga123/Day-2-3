Front End Developer Responsibilities: What Does a Front End Developer Do?

June 3, 2024 | Paridhi Wadhwani

What Does a Front End Developer Do?

A front end developer is responsible for linking the world of design as well as technology together. They create HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to develop web applications, ensuring an effective interaction between the product or service and the user. Everything you see, use, or click on a website—such as content, buttons, layout, images, navigation, and internal linking—is the work of a front end developer. Their role is to ensure users can access websites seamlessly across different platforms and browsers without bugs or errors.

Front end developers need strong analytical thinking, a solid technical foundation, and creativity to design interactive interfaces.

Front End Developer Responsibilities and Roles

  1. Prioritize products and features for user experience
  2. Convert website designs and link concepts with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  3. Ensure a functional, user-friendly interface
  4. Implement visual elements compatible with various platforms and browsers
  5. Follow best SEO practices
  6. Test website usability and fix errors or bugs
